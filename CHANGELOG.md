# Developer preview notes

## 0.1.0 — 4 November 2026

First public developer preview.

- The line around the notch for volume, brightness, charging, battery warnings (30, 20, 10, 5 percent) and timers.
- A frosted now-playing card with cover, progress, favorite (Apple Music), previous, play/pause, next and an output picker.
- A spinning record above the lock screen while music plays (experimental).
- Download progress for Safari, Chrome and Firefox.
- The shelf, with AirDrop and drag-out.
- Activities through the `n0tch` command, with Claude Code and Codex hooks.
- Focus indicator, timers and one Settings window.

Supports Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.
